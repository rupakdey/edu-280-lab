Lab foundation

Environment at a glance

One assigned POD contains a shared DIA router, two virtual ZTB gateways, three workload zones, and a ZPA App Connector. The topology below is conceptual.

Two virtual gateways

ZTB-1 (primary) and ZTB-2 (secondary) provide HA. Each pod includes two WAN, three LAN, one management, and one HA interface.

Three internal zones

Subnet10: Windows server; Subnet20: IoT Ubuntu; Subnet30: OT1 and OT2 Linux hosts.

Cloud connectivity

Shared direct-internet links connect the branch to Zscaler services; an App Connector supports private-application access through ZPA.

Getting started · Lab 0

Access your assigned POD

This source lab is an access checkpoint, not one of the nine numbered labs. Your POD ID, administrator password, domain and Skytap passcode are supplied separately for the session.

!
Use your assigned session details

Names and example IDs in screenshots are illustrative. Do not copy another student's POD, domain, access code, or password.

1 · Gather your credentials

Confirm your POD number (for example POD01), portal login ID in the form pod##-student@<FQDN>, session password, and Skytap passcode from the training email.

Experience Center

https://console.zscaler.com/

Skytap

https://zscaler.skytap-portal.com/

2 · Open the Skytap lab consoles

  1. Go to the assigned Skytap portal and enter the passcode from your course email.
  2. Confirm that the consoles include IoT, OT1, OT2, Server, ZCC Lab Machine, ZTB-1, and ZTB-2. Select a console to open it.
  3. If a console does not sign in automatically, use its provided Copy/Paste credential control; do not guess a password.

3 · Sign in to the Experience Center

  1. Open console.zscaler.com. Sign in using pod##-student@<FQDN>, replacing both placeholders with your assigned values.
  2. Enter the password supplied for your POD; confirm that the Experience Center dashboard opens.
✓
Access checkpoint complete

You can open ZTB-1 and ZTB-2 in Skytap and use Experience Center as the assigned administrator. Proceed to Lab 1.
